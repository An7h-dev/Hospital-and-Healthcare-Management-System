Accessibility

Our commitment to WCAG 2.1 Level AA

Accessibility statement

MediCare+ is designed to be usable by everyone, including people with visual, motor, hearing and cognitive disabilities. We aim to conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA.

Features in this build

  • Keyboard navigation — every control is reachable with the Tab key, with a visible focus outline.
  • Skip-to-content link — press Tab on any page to jump past the navigation.
  • Screen-reader support — semantic landmarks (<nav>, <main>), ARIA labels and table captions.
  • Not colour-only — every status badge pairs colour with an icon and text label.
  • Sufficient contrast — text meets a minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio (7:1 in High-contrast mode).
  • Three themes — Light, Dark and High-contrast, plus text-size controls (A-, A, A+).
  • Reduced motion — animations are disabled when the device requests reduced motion.
